What is Mavoo?

Mavoo is a social app for university students in the UK. Share your plans for tonight, see where your friends are heading, and find your people.

  • 🌙

    Tonight's plans

    Say where you're going tonight, and choose who sees it: people on Mavoo, or just your friends.

  • 🗺️

    Who's going

    See who's going to each bar and club on the map before you head out.

  • 🏠

    Your halls

    Meet people in your halls and make a group with your flat.

  • 🔒

    Never your live location

    Your exact location is never shown to anyone.

Starting in the North East

Mavoo launches first at three universities:

  • Newcastle University
  • Northumbria University
  • University of Sunderland

For students aged 18 and over.
